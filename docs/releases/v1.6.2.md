# MiSTerVision v1.6.2

## Release summary

Plex and Jellyfin now convert video to dimensions suited to the playback framebuffer, reducing unnecessary decoding work on MiSTer and SuperStation One. The change applies to movies, shows, and Live TV. Existing transcode limits remain caps, and source aspect ratio and local zoom behavior are preserved.

Validated with automated playback and display tests, MiSTer CRT and HDMI checks, and reporter testing on SuperStation One over HDMI. The reporter found Sonic and other shows smooth with the new sizing. This release also includes v1.6.1’s progressive video tearing fix and complete Plex artist album lists.

## Installation

Update through About or your existing Downloader registration. Manual installations use `mistervision-v1.6.2-progressive.zip`, which supports progressive and interlaced output. Install the matching client and player together. Existing settings and sign-in are preserved. No configuration change is required.
